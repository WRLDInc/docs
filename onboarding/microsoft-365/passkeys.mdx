A passkey lets you sign in to Microsoft 365 with your face, fingerprint, or device PIN instead of typing codes or approving push notifications. Passkeys can't be phished: they only work on the real Microsoft sign-in page, and only on a device you've registered.

<Info>
  WRLD staff need a passkey or another phishing-resistant sign-in method. You'll see a reminder at sign-in until you add one. You can postpone the reminder a few times; after that, setup is required.
</Info>

## Before you start

- You can already sign in to Microsoft 365 with your work account. If not, start with [Microsoft 365 initial sign-in](/onboarding/microsoft-365/initial-setup).
- Your phone runs iOS 17 / Android 14 or later and has **Microsoft Authenticator** installed and updated.
- Bluetooth is on for both your phone and computer if you set up from a computer.

## Choose your method

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Phone (Microsoft Authenticator)">
    Works everywhere and is the recommended first passkey.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open your security info">
        On your computer, go to [mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info](https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info) and sign in with your work account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add a passkey">
        Select **Add sign-in method**, then **Passkey**, then **Microsoft Authenticator**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Link your phone">
        Follow the prompts. When a QR code appears, scan it with your phone's camera and approve with your face, fingerprint, or phone PIN.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Name it">
        Give the passkey a name you'll recognize, such as **iPhone Authenticator**, and select **Done**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      You can also start on the phone: open Authenticator, tap your work account, and choose **Create a passkey**.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Windows PC (Windows Hello)">
    On a WRLD-managed Windows PC, Windows Hello for Business is your passkey. Set it up once and you won't be prompted for MFA on that PC.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Set up Windows Hello">
        Open **Settings** > **Accounts** > **Sign-in options** and set up **Facial recognition**, **Fingerprint**, or **PIN (Windows Hello)**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use a 6-digit PIN or longer">
        If Windows asks you to change your PIN, choose one with at least 6 digits.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in with Windows Hello">
        Lock and unlock your PC with Windows Hello. Microsoft 365 apps and Edge sign you in automatically.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mac (Platform SSO)">
    On a WRLD-managed Mac, your Mac's sign-in becomes your passkey through Platform SSO.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enroll the Mac">
        Install **Company Portal** from the App Store, open it, and sign in with your work account to enroll the Mac. If you get stuck, contact the helpdesk.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Register when prompted">
        When macOS shows **Registration required**, select **Register** and sign in with your work account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Approve with Touch ID">
        Approve with Touch ID or your Mac password. After that, Microsoft apps and Edge sign you in with Touch ID.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sign in with your passkey

1. Go to a Microsoft sign-in page and enter your work email.
2. Choose **Face, fingerprint, PIN, or security key**.
3. Approve on your device. On a computer without your passkey, choose your phone and scan the QR code.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the Passkey option">
    Update Microsoft Authenticator, then refresh the security info page. If the option still isn't there, contact the helpdesk.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The QR code scan doesn't connect">
    Turn on Bluetooth on both devices and keep your phone near the computer. Phone and computer don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I lost or replaced my phone">
    Contact the helpdesk. We'll issue a one-time Temporary Access Pass so you can sign in and add a passkey on your new phone, then remove the old one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
