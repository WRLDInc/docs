## How To Use The WRLD Help Desk Button

**Skip the screenshots and the facetime calls.** The BUTTON made by our partner Tier2 is here to help - we'll spare you the technical details, diagnostics and screengrabs it automatically provides to help us better help you and get straight to it.

<Card title="Don't have it installed?" icon="download" href="/support/tickets/help-button-install">
  Follow our quick install guide for Windows or macOS to get the Help Button on your device.
</Card>

## Where Can I Find It?

Look for our logo with **HELP**:

<img
  src="/images/help-button/help-button-icon.png"
  alt="WRLD Help Button icon"
  width="120"
/>

**On Windows:**

- **Your Taskbar!** Look for our logo with 'HELP' and simply click it
- **Your Desktop Icon** (Mixed in with other icons like the Recycle Bin)
- **By Pressing F1** (or the function key chosen during install)
- **By right-clicking** your system tray

<Frame caption="The Help Button pinned to the Windows taskbar">
  <img
    src="/images/help-button/taskbar.jpg"
    alt="WRLD Help Button in the Windows taskbar"
  />
</Frame>

**On macOS:** open the Help Button from your **Applications** folder, or press **⌘ Command + Space** and search for **Help**.

## How to Submit a Diagnostic Helpdesk Ticket

<Note>
**First time running?** You'll be prompted to enter your contact info (name and work email). This only happens once per user.
</Note>

When you click the Help Button, it first runs a quick set of diagnostics in the background:

<Frame caption="Diagnostics run automatically when you press the Help Button">
  <img
    src="/images/help-button/diagnostics.png"
    alt="WRLD Help Button running diagnostic tests"
  />
</Frame>

### Steps

1. **Click the WRLD Help Button** in your taskbar, desktop, or Applications folder
2. **Describe your issue** to the best of your ability, and let us know how urgent it is and how many people it affects

<Frame caption="Step 2 of 3: describe your problem or request">
  <img
    src="/images/help-button/ticket-details.jpg"
    alt="Problem and request details form"
  />
</Frame>

3. **Review AI suggestions** - You may be shown solutions to try first. Click "None of these, Continue" if they don't help.
4. **Confirm consent** - Acknowledge that you're not sending protected/confidential information, then click **Submit**

<Frame caption="Step 3 of 3: review, confirm consent, and submit">
  <img
    src="/images/help-button/submission-review.jpg"
    alt="Submission review and consent screen"
  />
</Frame>

5. **Receive your ticket number** - You'll get a confirmation with your ticket number so you can track progress
