## Open the Help Button on Windows

The WRLD Help Button is a small desktop app from our partner [Tier2 Tickets](https://tier2tickets.com) (Helpdesk Buttons), not a web form. Opening it takes screenshots and runs quick checks on your computer before you type a word. Use whichever shortcut is closest:

| Shortcut | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Link** | Click <a href="tier2tickets://run" target="_self" className="link">Open WRLD HELP</a> on this page. If your browser asks, choose **Open**. |
| **Function key** | Press **F1**, or the key chosen when the app was installed. |
| **Taskbar** | Click the **WRLD HELP** icon. Hovering over it shows *Helpdesk Button*. |
| **Start menu** | Press the **Windows** key, type `Helpdesk`, then press **Enter**. |
| **Run box** | Press **Windows + R**, enter `tier2tickets://run`, then press **Enter**. |
| **Desktop or tray** | Double-click the desktop icon, or right-click the icon near the clock. |

**On macOS:** open WRLD HELP from your **Applications** folder, or press **⌘ Command + Space** and search for **Help**.

<Note>
  If the link or shortcut does nothing, the app may not be installed on this computer yet. [Install the Help Button](/support/tickets/help-button-install), or use one of the options in [Get help now](#get-help-now).
</Note>

## What happens when you press it

The app runs its diagnostics first: screenshots, recent logs, network latency and packet loss, the Windows Event Viewer, and process usage. It takes a few seconds and needs nothing from you.

## Submit your ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your name and work email">
    The first time you use the Help Button, enter the name and work email we should reply to. You'll only see this once per Windows user.

    1. Your name and work email
    2. **Describe Issue** to continue

    <Frame caption="Step 1 of 3: who we should reply to">
      <img
        src="/images/help-button/help-button-step-1-contact.jpg"
        alt="Data collected and ready for submission, with name and email fields and a Describe Issue button"
      />
    </Frame>

    **Visit the portal** on the left opens the client portal, where you can check tickets you've already sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem or request">
    Tell us what's going on in your own words.

    1. What happened, or what you need
    2. Optional: ask us to connect remotely, call you, or book an on-site visit
    3. How urgent it is
    4. How many people it affects
    5. **Review Submission** to continue

    <Frame caption="Step 2 of 3: describe your problem or request">
      <img
        src="/images/help-button/help-button-step-2-details.jpg"
        alt="Problem and request details form with a description box, contact options, urgency, and scope"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a suggested fix, or continue">
    You may see a few fixes to try first. If none of them help, select **None of these, Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review, consent, and submit">
    Check the summary, then send it.

    1. Your contact details, description, and selections
    2. Leave **Submit Diagnostic Data** and **Submit Screen Shots** checked unless they show something private
    3. Confirm you're not sending protected or confidential information (required)
    4. **Submit**

    <Frame caption="Step 3 of 3: review, confirm consent, and submit">
      <img
        src="/images/help-button/help-button-step-3-review.jpg"
        alt="Submission review screen with the consent checkbox highlighted"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for your ticket number">
    You'll get a confirmation with your ticket number so you can follow progress. We'll reply by email or phone, depending on what you asked for.
  </Step>
</Steps>
