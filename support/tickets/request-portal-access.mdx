title: "Request Portal Access"
description: "Can't sign in to the WRLD client portal? Try these quick fixes, or ask us to set up your access."
---

You don't need portal access to get help. If you can't sign in to [portal.wrld.tech](https://portal.wrld.tech), you can still [call, email, or press WRLD HELP](/support/submit-ticket#get-help-now) while we sort out your account.

## Try these first

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal">
    Go to [portal.wrld.tech](https://portal.wrld.tech). Make sure the address bar shows `portal.wrld.tech`; older bookmarks may point to a previous address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your company account">
    Select **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft** and choose your **work** email, not a personal account. Not sure which one your company uses? See [Client Portal Login](/support/client-login#sign-in-with-your-company-email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset your password">
    If you sign in with an email address and password, use [Forgot password](https://portal.wrld.tech/portal/password/new) on the login page to get a reset link by email. Check your spam or junk folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## "Your portal account is not set up yet"

If the portal says **We could not sign you in: your portal account is not set up yet**, ask us to activate it with the Help Button. [Watch this part of the walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DRckhDcNAds&t=50) (0:50).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press WRLD HELP">
    Open the [WRLD Help Button](/support/tickets/help-button) and mention **portal login** or **activate portal account** in your description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for an email from the help desk">
    You'll get an invite or confirmation email from helpdesk{'@'}wrld.tech. You don't need to create a password unless you'd rather not use Google or Microsoft sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in again">
    Go back to [portal.wrld.tech](https://portal.wrld.tech) and select **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft** with the same work email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Still can't sign in? Request access

Portal accounts are set up by the WRLD team for your work email. Ask us and we'll create your account or fix the one you have.

    helpdesk{'@'}wrld.tech · subject: Portal access request
  </Card>
  <Card title="Call support" icon="phone" href="tel:+14698503968">
    (469) 850-3968 · talk to a technician about your access
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Include these details so we can set up your access in one step:

- Your full name and company name
- The work email you want to sign in with
- A phone number we can reach you at
- What happens when you try to sign in, such as an error message or a screenshot

<Info>
  Once your access is ready, we'll email you at your work address. Then go to [portal.wrld.tech](https://portal.wrld.tech) and sign in. Standard requests are handled Monday to Friday, 10am to 6pm CT.
</Info>
