Access your service tickets, review SLA dashboards, and manage co-managed IT resources through the WRLD client portal.

<Card title="Open the WRLD client portal" icon="arrow-up-right" href="https://portal.wrld.tech">
  Continue to the secure WRLD client portal to sign in with your company Google or Microsoft account, or your email address and password.
</Card>

<Info>
  The portal opens at `portal.wrld.tech` and displays WRLD Tech Co. branding. If you are not already signed in, you are taken to the portal login page.
</Info>

## Sign in with your company email

Use the same work email your company already uses for email and calendar. Pick the sign-in button that matches the platform your company runs on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal">
    Go to [portal.wrld.tech](https://portal.wrld.tech).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your company's platform">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Google Workspace">
        If your company email runs on Google (Gmail), select **Sign in with Google** and choose your company email account. Approve the prompt if Google asks you to confirm.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Microsoft 365">
        If your company email runs on Microsoft (Outlook / Office 365), select **Sign in with Microsoft** and enter your company email. Complete any multi-factor authentication (MFA) prompt your organization requires.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your dashboard">
    Once signed in, you're taken to your portal dashboard with your tickets and account details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Not sure which one your company uses? If you open your work email at `mail.google.com`, choose **Google**. If you use Outlook or `outlook.office.com`, choose **Microsoft**. Always sign in with your **company** email, not a personal account.
</Tip>

<Note>
  If your company doesn't use Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, sign in with your email address and portal password instead.
</Note>

## What you can access

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Service tickets" icon="ticket">
    View, update, and track all your open and resolved support requests in one place.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SLA dashboard" icon="chart-line">
    Monitor your service level agreement metrics, response times, and performance history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Co-managed IT" icon="server">
    Access shared IT management tools and visibility into your infrastructure.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Note>
  Can't sign in, or don't have a portal account yet? See [Request Portal Access](/support/tickets/request-portal-access), or email [helpdesk@wrld.tech](mailto:helpdesk@wrld.tech) to get access set up.
</Note>
